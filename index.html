Kisu Studio

A personal desktop tool of the science documentary channel @fatihoktemdocs.

Kisu Studio runs on one Windows computer and is used only by the channel owner. It edits the channel's own videos, reads the channel's own statistics (views, likes, watch time) from YouTube, Instagram and TikTok through their official APIs, and sends an approved video to the channel's own account (on TikTok as a draft that the owner finishes in the TikTok app). It is not offered to other people.

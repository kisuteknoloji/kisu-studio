Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Whose data

Kisu Studio only accesses the channel owner's own accounts (@fatihoktemdocs on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok). It does not access other people's accounts or data.

What is read

Profile basics (user name, display name), account statistics (followers, likes, video count) and each video's statistics (views, likes, comments, shares, watch time), and the list of the account's own videos.

Where it stays

Everything stays on the owner's Windows computer. Access tokens are kept in the Windows Credential Manager; statistics are kept as local files. Nothing is sent to any server other than the platform's own API, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Uploads

A video is uploaded only after the owner approves it; on TikTok it arrives as a draft in the owner's inbox and is posted by the owner in the TikTok app.

Removing access

The owner can revoke access at any time in each platform's app permissions; deleting the local files removes the stored statistics.
